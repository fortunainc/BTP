## Purpose

This file preserves credible BTP revenue opportunities so they can be evaluated when the network, evidence, trust, and buyer demand are ready. It is the canonical running list for monetization ideas.

Adding an idea here does not authorize immediate implementation. `BTP_PRODUCT_CONSTITUTION.md` controls the current beta. Revenue experiments should reuse existing infrastructure, begin manually where possible, and be promoted only after real demand is demonstrated.

## Revenue principles

- The trusted operator community and ordinary participation remain free.
- BTP charges for additional labor, valuable transactions, decision support, or enterprise outcomes—not for basic human help.
- Long-term revenue should come primarily from organizations, opportunity owners, and completed value creation rather than people experiencing layoffs or professional distress.
- Community content, identifiable activity, employment status, and private conversations are never sold.
- Paid research, introductions, and expert work require specific consent.
- Test demand manually before building automation, dashboards, marketplaces, payment systems, or production prediction infrastructure. Design permissions, outcome definitions, and feasible data collection early.

## Priority order

The tiers below describe dependencies, not a rigid company sequence. Paid expertise and structured operator research/evaluations are parallel early commercial paths. Prediction is a core long-term ambition: dataset design and feasibility work begin early, while customer-facing forecasts require validation. Availability and opportunities remain meaningful expansion rather than the primary company identity.


### Tier 1 — Validate with the current or immediately available infrastructure

1. Interview preparation
2. Private expert consultation
3. Document or workflow review
4. Defined project help

These monetize dedicated labor and can be founder-mediated without building a marketplace.

### Tier 2 — Validate once a verified, opted-in network exists

1. BTP Operator Intelligence research sprints
2. Expert calls
3. Verified opportunity introductions
4. Contract, fractional, and project matching
5. Deep protocol, product, or workflow reviews

These use the network as a supply and trust advantage. Begin manually and measure repeat demand.

### Tier 3 — Introduce after sufficient structured evidence and recurring demand

1. Clinical Trial Operator Signal Reports
2. Operational-risk monitoring
3. Benchmarks
4. Custom privacy-safe research
5. Employer access and transition programs
6. Role-specific training based on demonstrated recurring needs

### Tier 4 — Long-term data and intelligence products

1. Calibrated operational forecasts (core strategic ambition; early data design, evidence-gated release)
2. Monitored risk portfolios
3. Enterprise intelligence subscriptions
4. Aggregate licensed feeds and APIs

These require independent, outcome-labeled longitudinal data, methodology governance, and privacy-safe cohorts.

## Future consideration: BTP Impact Credits

**Decision status:** Preserve for deeper evaluation after the basic beta loop works. Do not implement as cryptocurrency.

BTP may introduce closed, non-transferable **Impact Credits** to recognize and reward useful contributions. This is primarily a retention and contribution mechanism, not a standalone revenue stream. It may support revenue by increasing answer supply, improving outcome capture, reducing the cost of community activation, and allowing members to apply earned value toward selected paid services.

### Proposed principle

> Members earn useful internal value when their experience demonstrably helps another verified operator.

Credits should be awarded for demonstrated usefulness rather than raw activity. Illustrative—not final—events include:

| Event | Illustrative treatment |
|---|---:|
| Answer passes safety review | +1 |
| Asker marks the answer helpful | +3 |
| Independent verified operator corroborates it | +2 |
| Asker reports applying the answer | +5 |
| Reported outcome confirms the answer helped | +10 |
| Useful clarification improves the case | +1 |
| Accurate report protects the community | +2 |
| Contribution is removed, reversed, or credibly disputed | Withhold or reverse credits |

The primary emotional reward should be an impact receipt such as:

> **Your answer helped another clinical-trial professional resolve a real problem.**

### Potential utility

- Boost an unanswered question for relevant-expert routing
- Request a second experienced perspective
- Receive a limited discount on interview preparation or private help
- Access an early Operator Signal briefing
- Become eligible for selected paid Operator Intelligence panels, subject to fit and separate consent
- Receive invitations to small expert roundtables
- Nominate or invite another trusted professional
- Unlock limited, safety-appropriate community privileges over time

### Keep separate

1. **Impact Credits** — spendable internal utility for demonstrated contribution.
2. **Private trust signals** — non-purchasable safety, routing, and quality signals.
3. **Cash compensation** — real payment for paid research, interviews, consulting, or project work.

Credits must never substitute for cash when a customer is paying BTP for a member's labor or expertise.

### Guardrails

- No crypto, blockchain, wallet, tradable token, or speculative value.
- No purchase or transfer of credits between members.
- No public leaderboard, follower hierarchy, or popularity contest.
- Do not award meaningful value merely for posting an answer.
- Do not equate credit balance with professional credibility.
- Do not disadvantage members who primarily need help.
- Apply rate limits, reversal rules, collusion detection, and moderation.
- Do not let credits buy professional visibility or circumvent matching quality.
- Clearly disclose that credits have no cash value and may change during testing.

### Validation sequence

1. Use existing impact receipts, helpfulness, and outcome notifications during early beta.
2. After approximately 50–100 active members, test visible but initially non-redeemable credits with a limited cohort.
3. Compare answer quality, answer rate, time to useful response, outcome completion, and 7/30-day retention against a control or prior baseline.
4. If quality remains strong, test only two redemptions: an unanswered-question boost and a limited private-help/interview-prep credit.
5. Expand utility only when behavior demonstrates that credits improve genuine help rather than volume or gaming.

Stop or redesign the system if it increases low-quality answers, reciprocal voting, status anxiety, privacy concerns, or moderation burden.

## Evaluation template for new revenue ideas

Every future idea added to this file should record:

1. Customer and payer
2. Painful decision or job being solved
3. Why BTP has an advantage
4. Manual validation method
5. Minimum proof before building
6. Pricing hypothesis
7. Member benefit
8. Trust, privacy, legal, and conflict risks
9. Required infrastructure
10. Continue/stop criteria

## Anti-drift rule

The immediate goal remains bringing the current beta to verified operators and completing useful **Ask → Answer → Apply → Outcome** cycles. Preserve promising revenue ideas here, but do not let an unvalidated future stream delay user acquisition, trust formation, outcome collection, or the existing infrastructure plan.
