## Strategic thesis

BTP begins as the trusted place where verified clinical-trial professionals can ask questions they cannot comfortably ask at work and receive practical answers from people who have handled similar situations.

That community is the trust-formation layer for a larger business:

> **Verified life-sciences talent, available now, connected through people who know their work.**

The initial commercial wedge is a private, trusted availability network for experienced clinical-development and clinical-trial professionals during layoffs, transitions, quiet exploration, career returns, and industry instability.

## What BTP is building toward

1. A safe professional network where verified members can participate without public exposure.
2. A trusted availability graph that knows who is open to what, under what visibility conditions, and who can credibly vouch for their work.
3. A consent-based opportunity and introduction layer.
4. A marketplace for permanent, contract, fractional, project, advisory, expert-call, board, speaking, collaboration, and team-formation opportunities.
5. Privacy-safe workforce intelligence only when sufficient consent, density, and governance exist.

## Initial market

- Clinical development and clinical-trial execution
- Initial geography: United States, while permitting experienced global contributors where verification and legal boundaries are clear
- Initial members: site professionals, CRAs, CTMs, study/program leaders, clinical science, regulatory, quality, data, statistics, medical writing, safety, startup, feasibility, recruitment, vendor/alliance, systems, and relevant functional leadership

Expansion into broader biotech, pharma, medtech, healthcare, research sites, service providers, and adjacent functions comes only after density is proven in the initial market.

## Product sequencing

### Stage 1 — Current private beta

- Verified, invite-only membership
- Identity-protected questions and experience-based answers
- Follow, helpfulness, outcomes, reporting, moderation, and private-help requests
- Founder-mediated trust and expert matching

### Stage 2 — First verified cohort

- Learn recurring professional, operational, and transition needs from 25–50 trusted members
- Validate return behavior, trusted invitations, useful answers, private-help demand, and willingness to disclose availability privately
- Offer interview preparation manually through the existing private-help flow

### Stage 3 — Availability pilot

- Progressive capability profile
- Private availability status and desired engagement types
- Granular visibility and consent controls
- Trusted invitations and direct-work attestations
- Founder-curated introductions

### Stage 4 — First opportunity transactions

- Verified opportunity intake
- Consent before identity disclosure
- Introduction acceptance or decline
- Outcome tracking
- Defined paid work, interview preparation, expert help, and selected permanent-role introductions

### Stage 5 — Marketplace validation

- Repeat opportunity owners
- Repeat member participation
- Completed engagements or hires
- Transparent marketplace or success fees
- System-supported matching informed by real outcomes, not premature AI scoring

### Stage 6 — Scaled trust network

- Cohort-based layoff response
- Delegated verification and moderation with least-privilege access
- Team formation and broader opportunity types
- Employer-side tools and aggregated intelligence with separate consent

## Interview preparation offering

**BTP Interview Prep** is an approved early service because it solves a frequent, immediate problem: experienced people often know the work but do not communicate their value effectively in interviews.

Initial format:

1. Private intake covering the role, interview stage, and member's real experience.
2. Role-specific mock interview with a verified interviewer who has genuine interview or hiring experience.
3. Structured feedback on examples, clarity, judgment, executive presence, cross-functional communication, and unanswered risks.
4. A concise practice plan and optional follow-up session.
5. Private outcome check after the interview.

The service can begin manually with Sasha as an interviewer without turning BTP into a founder-dependent coaching business. Over time, verified members with credible interviewing experience may opt in to provide sessions, with BTP facilitating quality, scheduling, payment, and private feedback.

No proprietary employer questions, candidate information, confidential scorecards, or employer-specific inside information may be requested or shared. Participation and outcomes remain private.

## Non-negotiable differentiation

- Not a generic job board
- Not a public “open to work” feed
- Not a recruiter résumé database
- Not an uncontrolled consultant directory
- Not a popularity-driven social network
- Not a traditional recruiting agency dependent on constant cold outreach
- Not a layoff community that monetizes distress

BTP's defensible asset is:

> **Verified identity + structured capability + current availability + credible professional relationships + consented introductions + outcomes.**

## Trust and privacy rules

- Availability is never inferred from layoff news or exposed without explicit consent.
- Members control whether they are unavailable, quietly exploring, open to conversations, actively seeking, or available for projects, fractional work, advisory work, or expert calls.
- Identity disclosure to an employer or opportunity owner requires member consent.
- Attestations must reflect direct working knowledge or a specific capability; generic endorsement farming is prohibited.
- No public ratings, follower counts, popularity rankings, or uncontrolled direct messaging.
- Employer, recruiter, opportunity, and member misconduct must be reportable and auditable.
- Data products must be aggregated, privacy-preserving, purpose-limited, and separately consented.

## Commercial principle

Do not charge someone simply because she has been laid off. Optional individualized interview preparation and private help may carry transparent fees because they involve dedicated labor. The durable revenue model should develop primarily from verified opportunities, employer access to consented talent, transactions, successful engagements, transition programs, and later employer-side tools.

## Anti-drift test

Before expanding BTP, ask whether the proposed work strengthens at least one of:

1. Safe member participation
2. Verified capability
3. Current, consented availability
4. Credible professional trust relationships
5. Relevant opportunities or introductions
6. Meaningful outcomes or aligned transactions

If it does none of these—or merely recreates a commodity LinkedIn, job-board, recruiter, Slack, or marketplace feature—it should not be built.
