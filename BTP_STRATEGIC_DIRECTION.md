# BTP Strategic Direction

**Status:** Aligned October 16, 2026 with the [canonical final vision](docs/vision/BTP_FINAL_VISION.md).

## Controlling direction

BTP connects clinical-trial professionals to practical help and trusted expertise, then develops paid operator research and permissioned operational intelligence, including validated prediction products. The company is broader than the first app or any fixed list of assets.

The previous October 13 availability-first company thesis is superseded. Private availability, consented opportunities, interview preparation, and credible working relationships remain valuable components; they do not define BTP as a staffing company.

## Connected paths

1. **Member value:** Free Ask -> Answer -> Apply -> Outcome participation, private help, and useful professional relationships.
2. **Commercial value:** Optional paid expertise and interview preparation; expert calls, structured research, and independent vendor/product evaluations with separately opted-in, compensated professionals.
3. **Data value:** Design permissioned longitudinal datasets early; develop repeatable reports, benchmarks, early warnings, and narrow prediction products when evidence supports their use.

These paths may be validated in parallel without rebuilding the beta or requiring an enterprise platform first. Research/evaluation is a principal early B2B path to test alongside private help, not an instruction to abandon member usefulness.

## Expansion

Preserve consented introductions, contract/fractional/project matching, selected placement and employer programs as demand-led options. Preserve the wider portfolio in [the revenue registry](BTP_FUTURE_REVENUE_STREAMS.md). Do not make outsourced trial execution or generic recruitment the default company model.

Prediction is a core long-term ambition, not an incidental add-on. Organize data now; select the first operational target using operator feedback, outcome access, and a buyer decision. A module or dataset does not establish predictive validity. The [data doctrine](BTP_DATA_INTELLIGENCE_DOCTRINE_2026-10-12.md) supplies supporting controls subject to the canonical vision.

## Product and trust

The [product constitution](BTP_PRODUCT_CONSTITUTION.md) governs the current beta. Preserve the existing web/backend and mobile work. Professional membership, safe participation, useful answers, outcome follow-up, and consent remain foundational. Ordinary community participation is not consent to paid research or commercial model training.

Public website copy serves research professionals. Detailed commercialization, benchmarking, and prediction strategy belongs in strategy materials and the protected investor briefing. Keep the repository public and secrets private.

## Anti-drift rule

Read the canonical vision before changing strategy or roadmap. Distinguish implemented capabilities, approved direction, proposed experiments, and demonstrated results. Do not copy a financial model's valuation, staffing, financing, or launch assumptions into the strategy as commitments. Record a genuine founder-directed change in the canonical source, regenerate its PDF, and reconcile dependent documents.
