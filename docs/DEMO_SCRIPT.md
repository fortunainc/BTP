# 10-minute demo script

For investors and CTO candidates. Use real seeded content, never fabricated member activity. Before demoing: at least 15 real questions with answers, one poll with votes, and two signed-in accounts (founder and a test member).

| Min | Show | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0-1 | Homepage | "Clinical research operators know first when trials break, but they can't say it at work. BTP is where they can." |
| 1-2 | `/privacy` | Identity separation: handle and broad role only; no employer contact; no reporting tasks. |
| 2-4 | Test member: ask a question (`/situations/new`) | Redaction protects identifying details; founding responders are alerted to give a fast first answer. |
| 4-5 | Founder: answer it | The asker gets a role-labelled alert (for example "A verified site professional answered your question") within minutes. The label comes from the answerer's approved role; check the founder account's role first. |
| 5-6 | Test member: mark "It worked" | The helper is told their answer worked. Reactions: helpful, same here, saved. |
| 6-7 | `/polls` | One tap, then peer results once at least 5 members have answered. Aggregated poll results will feed the monthly Site Operations Index (next build step). |
| 7-8 | `/help` | Private help: interview prep and consultations, matched only with recorded consent from both sides. |
| 8-9 | `/admin/engagement` | The metrics we run the beta on: time to first answer, answered within 2 hours, outcomes reported, members who ask and answer. |
| 9-10 | The business | Community first, patterns second: members see insights first; planning teams buy aggregate patterns (enrollment blockers, workload, process failures), never a named site, study or person. First focus: enrollment reality. |

**For CTO candidates, add 10 minutes:** `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and `docs/STATE_OF_THE_CODEBASE.md`; walk through CI and what they would own.

**Do not:** show production data from real members without consent, imply traction that doesn't exist, or show the admin email field.
