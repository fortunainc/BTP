# Operator research sprint - offer draft (v0.1)

Date: 2026-10-16. Status: approved direction (paid expertise and operator research); pricing is a hypothesis to test.

## What the buyer gets

A focused answer to one product, protocol or workflow question from verified clinical-trial professionals with relevant experience.

- 8-15 structured interviews (site, CRA, study management roles as relevant), optional short pulse survey
- Decision brief: findings, where roles disagree, recommendations, limitations
- Method appendix: who participated (roles and experience bands only), questions asked, how findings were synthesized

## Who buys

Clinical technology and service vendors (product, research, marketing leads), emerging biotech clinical operations, investors and consultants doing diligence.

## Process

1. Scoping call: one decision the buyer needs to make; success criteria.
2. Recruit separately consented, paid participants from the BTP network; screen for real exposure.
3. Interviews and synthesis.
4. Brief and readout.

Target timeline: 2-4 weeks (to be confirmed by the first projects).

## Independence and information rules

- The buyer pays for the process, not a favorable finding. Negative findings are kept.
- Evaluation questions and rubric are agreed before interviews start.
- Participants share experience, not employer-confidential information, protocol documents or patient data.
- Participant identities are not disclosed to the buyer unless the participant separately agrees.

## Pricing hypothesis

$7,500-$15,000 per sprint depending on scope; participant honoraria $150-250 per interview included. Deeper multi-role reviews: $20,000-$40,000. Validate with the first 2-3 buyers.

## Intake questions

What decision will this inform, and by when? Which roles and settings matter? What would change your plan? Who else will see the results?
