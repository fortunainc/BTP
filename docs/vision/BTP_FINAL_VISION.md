# BTP | Final Company Vision

Version 1.0 | Locked October 16, 2026 | Founder-directed source of truth

## 1. The company we are building

BTP (Behind the Protocol) connects clinical-trial professionals so they can solve problems, share practical experience, and access trusted expertise. Over time, BTP will turn separately permissioned, structured operator evidence and measured outcomes into research, operational intelligence, and validated prediction products that help the industry make better decisions.

The ambition is a durable, highly valuable company built on professional trust, useful expertise, and evidence that improves clinical-trial execution. BTP is broader than its initial app, its first services, or any fixed list of assets. The starting product is deliberately focused; the company vision is not limited to it.

### Why it exists

Clinical-trial workers hold valuable practical knowledge that often stays inside one team or leaves with an employee. Professionals need a trusted place to brainstorm, ask difficult questions, and learn from people who understand the work. BTP helps that experience remain useful across careers and organizational boundaries without taking confidential employer information.

The shared north star is helping patients by supporting the development of effective treatments as quickly as sound science, patient safety, and responsible research allow. BTP contributes by helping the people doing the work make better operational decisions; it does not promise faster approvals or better patient outcomes from an app.

### Who it serves

Members include site teams, coordinators, research nurses, investigators, CRAs, clinical operations and study leaders, and relevant sponsor, CRO, vendor, regulatory, quality, safety, data, and other research professionals. Sites are a central constituency, not simply a source of data.

Organizational buyers may include clinical technology and service vendors, sponsors and emerging biotech, CROs, site networks, investors, consultants, and other qualified decision-makers. A member, a research participant, and a paying buyer are distinct roles with distinct permissions.

### The member promise

Get practical help from people who understand clinical research. Ask in context, compare approaches, apply what is appropriate, and return with what happened. Ordinary community participation remains free. Identity is verified privately and protected in discussions; absolute anonymity is not promised.

## 2. How the business fits together

### Professional trust and support

The core loop is Ask -> Answer -> Apply -> Outcome. Useful answers, follow-up, private help, and credible professional relationships give people reasons to return. Interview preparation, consultations, workflow reviews, and defined project help extend that value when dedicated labor is needed.

### Paid expertise and operator research

BTP can connect buyers with relevant, separately opted-in professionals for expert calls, research sprints, independent product evaluations, and deeper workflow or protocol reviews conducted within appropriate information boundaries. Professionals are compensated for paid participation. Buyers pay for useful evidence and an independent evaluation process, never a guaranteed favorable finding.

Research and vendor evaluations are a principal early B2B path to test alongside private help. They are also a bridge toward repeatable intelligence products. Manual delivery is appropriate initially; recurring demand and delivery economics determine what to automate.

### Intelligence and prediction

Permissioned observations, context, interventions, corroboration, and verified outcomes can support reports, comparable benchmarks, early warnings, and eventually calibrated forecasts. Prediction modeling is a core long-term ambition. Data design and feasibility work begin early, in parallel with member and commercial validation; selling predictions depends on demonstrated performance and decision value.

### The revenue portfolio

Near-term candidates are optional paid private help and interview preparation, expert calls, research sprints, independent vendor/product evaluations, and deeper operational reviews. BTP earns clearly disclosed service, project, or facilitation fees.

Recurring opportunities include research subscriptions, operator signal reports, benchmarks, operational-risk monitoring, validated forecast subscriptions, monitored portfolios, and appropriately governed aggregate feeds or APIs. Project revenue is not recurring revenue merely because a customer might return.

Private availability, consented introductions, contract/fractional/project opportunities, selected placement fees, employer programs, and transition support remain meaningful expansion paths. They do not define BTP as a staffing company. Training, briefings, roundtables, and carefully governed sponsorship remain secondary options where members benefit.

These are routes to validate, not claims of current sales. The revenue registry preserves the broader portfolio. BTP need not own trial execution, become a CRO, or launch every stream to succeed. Its advantage must grow through trusted participation, repeat customers, useful outcomes, and reusable evidence rather than permanent dependence on founder labor.

## 3. The prediction and data vision

### What BTP aims to predict

Start with operational events that a buyer can act on: activation delays, amendment implementation delays, enrollment shortfalls, workflow burden, or vendor-related execution friction. These are candidate targets, not a commitment to build all of them. Choose the first target using operator feedback, an identifiable buyer decision, access to reliable outcomes, and achievable data collection.

Predicting patient outcomes, drug efficacy, individual employability, or individual performance is outside the agreed initial scope. A future expansion into a materially different clinical use requires a separate strategy and evidence decision; another company's patient-prediction results do not validate BTP.

### How the dataset develops

Use a combination of sources where appropriate: separately consented longitudinal operator panels, structured paid research with explicit reuse rights, authorized partner records, and suitable licensed or public data. Community discussions reveal needs and hypotheses; membership and ordinary posting do not automatically authorize model training or commercial reuse.

For each prediction use case, define the outcome, prediction time, decision horizon, permitted inputs, and the record that establishes what actually happened. Preserve when the event occurred, when it was reported, when BTP could have known the information, and when the outcome was confirmed. Record unresolved and missing outcomes separately from successful or negative outcomes.

Backend organization should preserve source, permissions, permitted uses, data versions, corrections, linkage boundaries, and retention. Separate account identity, community narratives, research records, model features, and external outputs. Do not assume that combining records is permitted because each record can be accessed individually.

### What makes it valuable

Volume alone is not a moat. Value comes from relevant coverage across independent organizations, reliable follow-up, comparable definitions, trustworthy labels, lawful reuse, and evidence that the signals improve a real decision. Reports and models must disclose uncertainty and sample limitations. Convenience samples cannot establish industry-wide prevalence by themselves.

### How prediction earns its place

Begin with one narrow target and a simple baseline. Evaluate on later observations and independent groups where appropriate, prevent leakage, assess calibration and practical decision value, and test silently before relying on forecasts in paid workflows. Monitor drift and performance after release.

No fixed number of members or cases automatically proves validity. Study design, base rates, independence, uncertainty, and the consequence of an error determine what evidence is enough. Small pilots establish data feasibility; they do not automatically establish a sellable predictive model.

Member trust is the foundation: no sale of identifiable member activity or ordinary conversations, no undisclosed reuse, no purchased favorable evaluations, and no requirement to join paid research to remain a member. Correction, withdrawal, deletion, and retraining implications must be defined for each research program and represented accurately.

## 4. Execution without drift

### Build from what exists

Preserve and improve the existing web backend, admin tools, and Expo/React Native member app. The implementation baseline includes questions, answers, outcomes, private-help workflows, membership controls, and moderation. Implementation does not prove adoption, retention, paid demand, operational readiness, or predictive performance.

The known native release is an invitation-only TestFlight beta. Do not describe that as public App Store availability without new verification. A forecasting module, taxonomy, schema, or SDK dependency is not a validated prediction product.

### Run three connected learning tracks

Member value: bring relevant professionals into the existing help loop. Measure useful answers, time to help, repeat participation, outcome follow-up, and whether members help one another without repeated founder intervention.

Commercial value: test a bounded private-help or research/evaluation offer. Measure willingness to pay, completion, participant benefit, delivery effort, contribution after real costs, and repeat or expanded purchases. Pricing, headcount, and a fundraising ask remain adjustable commercial choices.

Data value: define a first candidate prediction decision and test whether permissioned observations and dependable outcomes can be collected. Start dataset design now without turning ordinary participation into a burdensome survey or delaying the useful beta.

These tracks can progress in parallel at an appropriate scale. Evidence determines expansion. A research-services business is a useful entry point only if BTP deliberately builds repeatability, reusable methods, recurring demand, and permissioned evidence alongside delivery.

### Product boundaries

Preserve private opportunities and interview preparation. Do not default to a generic job board, public talent directory, outsourced trial-startup company, general consultancy, or broad social network. Do not restart functioning infrastructure or reactivate dormant modules simply because they exist.

Use professional-facing public messaging about practical support, site value, identity protection, and follow-through. Keep detailed monetization, benchmarking, data strategy, and forecasting discussion in the investor briefing and strategy materials, not the public marketing page. The repository remains public; this vision contains no credentials, private member information, or confidential financial plan.

### Financial discipline

Financial forecasts and valuations are scenarios, not parts of the locked vision. Earlier Claude models do not establish actual traction, a current valuation, an approved raise, hiring commitments, or guaranteed outcomes. Preserve the ambition while changing assumptions when evidence changes. Do not invent costs or demand merely to produce a desired valuation.

## 5. Source of truth and change control

### Authority

This document is the canonical company vision as of October 16, 2026. It supersedes older strategy language making talent availability the primary company direction, treating prediction as an incidental add-on, or requiring BTP to become an outsourced trial-execution business.

The product constitution controls current beta scope subject to this vision. The strategic-direction document explains expansion; the data doctrine governs research and prediction; the revenue registry preserves candidate business models. None may silently override this vision.

Claude V2-revised and its website review are analytical inputs. V3 is a reference comparison, not the controlling strategy. Earlier documents remain useful history where consistent. A model's proposed numbers, a consultant's recommendation, or generated copy is not a founder decision.

### Rules for every contributor and AI assistant

Read this vision before strategy, product, investor-copy, or roadmap work. Label substantive statements as implemented, approved direction, proposed experiment, or demonstrated result. Verify implementation and business claims against their actual sources rather than repeating another report.

A proposed change should strengthen member usefulness and trust, paid value from expertise, or the quality and usefulness of permissioned evidence. Identify the beneficiary, the result being tested, and the simplest useful implementation. Prefer reversible progress over unnecessary approval steps.

Routine copy, documentation, quality fixes, and implementation within this direction can proceed under the user's existing authorization. A genuine change in company identity, data-use permissions, or clinical prediction scope must be surfaced explicitly rather than smuggled into a rewrite.

Update this canonical source first when the founder changes the vision, record the reason and date, regenerate its PDF, and reconcile dependent documents and investor copy. Keep credentials and plaintext investor briefing material out of the public repository. A historical document is not controlling merely because it says 'locked.'
