# BTP Data Intelligence Doctrine

**Status:** Locked strategic doctrine  
**Locked:** October 12, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles)  
**Relationship to existing source of truth:** `BTP_PRODUCT_CONSTITUTION.md` continues to control the beta product. `BTP_STRATEGIC_DIRECTION.md` continues to control the expansion from trusted community to availability and opportunity network. This document controls how BTP develops its long-term data, intelligence, and forecasting asset. It does not authorize a restart or expansion of the current beta.

## Long-term thesis

BTP can become the trusted human-signal network that detects where clinical trials are breaking before conventional systems can see it.

The operating model is:

> **Verified operators + structured operational signals + corroboration + measured outcomes over time = clinical-trial operational intelligence and, eventually, defensible forecasts.**

BTP must earn this position through member trust and high-quality longitudinal data. It must not market itself as a prediction platform before its historical evidence supports calibrated predictions.

## Why BTP can create differentiated intelligence

Traditional clinical-trial data sources primarily capture official, retrospective events: registrations, milestones, enrollment figures, protocol amendments, regulatory disclosures, and company announcements.

BTP can capture privacy-safe precursor signals from the people doing the work:

- Repeated questions arising across independent organizations
- Trial stage or workflow where friction occurs
- Roles and organization types experiencing the problem
- Workarounds and interventions operators attempt
- Agreement, disagreement, and corroboration among verified practitioners
- Time to resolution
- Whether the proposed approach worked
- Escalation into private help
- Recurrence across unrelated organizations, functions, and time periods

Questions alone are not a defensible dataset. The strategic asset is the complete signal-to-outcome loop.

## Required data loop

Every case should progressively support six structured components without forcing burdensome forms on beta users:

1. **Signal** — What problem or question appeared?
2. **Context** — Where in the clinical-trial lifecycle did it occur?
3. **Exposure** — Relevant role, organization type, geography, phase, therapeutic area, and functional domain, recorded only at a privacy-safe level.
4. **Intervention** — What did the operator try?
5. **Outcome** — What happened after the intervention?
6. **Timing and corroboration** — How long did resolution take, and how many independent verified operators observed the same pattern?

The existing **Ask → Answer → Apply → Report what happened** loop is therefore the foundation of BTP's future intelligence business. Outcome capture is strategically critical and must remain easy, useful, and non-punitive.

## Forecast categories BTP may pursue

BTP should begin with expensive operational events, not drug efficacy, patient outcomes, individual performance, or medical predictions.

Potential future forecasts include:

- Probability that a study misses a site-activation target
- Startup conditions likely to delay first-patient-in
- Probability that enrollment misses plan within a defined indication, phase, or geography
- Vendor categories becoming recurrent execution bottlenecks
- Operational burden or failure risk created by protocol design or amendments
- Emerging inspection, quality, data-query, or compliance pressure
- Workforce or capability shortages likely to affect execution
- Operational problems accelerating across independent organizations

The most credible initial intelligence product is:

> **BTP Operational Risk Intelligence: what clinical-trial execution problems are emerging, how broadly they are appearing, and what is likely to happen next.**

## Evidence thresholds

These are planning thresholds, not guarantees of statistical validity. Each external claim must also consider data quality, cohort diversity, independence, selection bias, base rates, and confidence intervals.

| Evidence level | Working threshold | Permitted use |
|---|---:|---|
| Early internal signal | 3–5 verified operators across at least 3 independent organizations | Flag internally for monitoring; no prevalence claim |
| Strong qualitative insight | 15–30 relevant, consistently classified cases | Describe what participating operators are experiencing; do not claim industry prevalence |
| Directional benchmark | 75–150 comparable cases | Cautious comparisons across sufficiently populated roles, stages, or organization types |
| Commercial intelligence | 300–500 structured cases from approximately 100–250 verified contributors | Aggregated trend reports, pain-point maps, and clearly qualified early warnings |
| Initial forecasting research | 1,000+ comparable cases with measurable outcomes | Build and back-test narrow models; no customer-facing probability until calibration is demonstrated |
| Defensible prediction product | Approximately 3,000–10,000+ high-quality, outcome-labeled cases across time and independent organizations | Calibrated, time-bound probabilities for narrowly defined operational events, with methodology and performance disclosed |

For an operational signal to be considered commercially important, BTP should normally seek:

- Evidence from at least 5 independent organizations
- Corroboration from approximately 10–20 verified operators
- A measurable change over a defined time window
- Consistent context or a clearly defined cohort
- No single organization or contributor disproportionately driving the result
- For predictive claims, evidence that the signal precedes and predicts a measurable event out of sample

## First meaningful network milestone

BTP does not need a mass audience before producing useful intelligence. A credible initial target is:

- 250 verified operators
- 500 substantive questions or cases
- 1,500 useful responses
- 150 measured case outcomes
- 10–20 recurring operational problems corroborated across independent organizations

At this threshold, BTP may be able to publish a carefully qualified **Clinical Trial Operator Signal Report**. This report should distinguish observation from inference, disclose sample limitations, and avoid identifying organizations or individuals.

Two hundred highly relevant, verified contributors can be more valuable than 20,000 passive accounts. Contribution quality, independence, diversity, and measured outcomes matter more than raw user totals.

## Privacy and trust requirements

No intelligence product may undermine the community that created it.

- Never sell identifiable posts, activity, questions, answers, employment status, or member behavior.
- Never expose company-level allegations, confidential study information, patient information, or individual performance conclusions.
- Do not infer or reveal a member's employer, availability, job search, layoff status, or identity.
- External outputs must be aggregated, de-identified, purpose-limited, and governed by clear terms and consent.
- Sensitive segments should normally require at least 10 independent contributors and sufficient organizational diversity before display.
- Quotes require explicit permission and additional review for re-identification risk.
- Enterprise buyers purchase analysis and aggregated signals—not access to community conversations or member identities.
- Models, definitions, material methodology changes, and forecast revisions must be versioned and auditable.
- Customers must be told what a forecast measures, its time horizon, relevant limitations, and historical performance.

## Business progression

1. **Trusted community** — verified operators ask, answer, follow, and report outcomes.
2. **Structured recurring problems** — BTP organizes privacy-safe patterns without burdening participation.
3. **Operator signal reports** — qualified, aggregated observations establish an intelligence product and methodology.
4. **Benchmarks and early warnings** — sufficiently dense cohorts support directional comparisons and monitored signals.
5. **Forecasting research** — narrow models are built and back-tested using outcome-labeled historical cases.
6. **Operational forecasts** — calibrated probabilities describe measurable events, time horizons, evidence, and uncertainty.
7. **Enterprise intelligence** — subscriptions, monitored risk portfolios, research, and APIs are offered without exposing members or raw community content.

Potential buyers include biotech and pharmaceutical sponsors, CROs, research-site organizations, vendors, investors, insurers, consultants, and other organizations making clinical-development capital and operating decisions. Buyer demand must be validated before building enterprise dashboards.

## Commercial principle

BTP's long-term intelligence revenue should come from organizations that benefit from earlier, better operational decisions. Members should receive a safe and useful professional community; they should not become an unknowingly exploited dataset.

Possible future revenue includes:

- Paid Clinical Trial Operator Signal reports
- Recurring operational-risk intelligence subscriptions
- Custom, privacy-safe research
- Narrow benchmark products
- Monitored risk portfolios and alerts
- Licensed aggregate data feeds or APIs after governance and density are sufficient

No revenue model authorizes access to identifiable member activity or raw conversations.

## Anti-drift rules

This doctrine does **not** change the immediate beta priority:

> Help a verified clinical-trial professional safely obtain a useful answer to a specific question and report what happened.

Therefore:

- Do not delay the beta to build predictive models, enterprise dashboards, complex taxonomies, or research infrastructure.
- Do not turn ordinary participation into a long survey.
- Do not claim industry prevalence from convenience samples.
- Do not confuse engagement metrics with independent operational evidence.
- Do not train customer-facing prediction models on unreviewed free text alone.
- Do not predict individual employability, individual performance, medical outcomes, drug efficacy, or patient outcomes.
- Do not reactivate broad enterprise-intelligence features merely because dormant infrastructure exists.
- Do preserve structured context, corroboration, time, intervention, and outcome data needed for future analysis.
- Do make outcome reporting rewarding and easy.
- Do validate every commercial intelligence product with actual buyers before investing in scaled infrastructure.

## Decision test

Before adding a data field, taxonomy, model, dashboard, or report, ask:

1. Does it improve the current member experience or future signal-to-outcome quality?
2. Can it be collected without creating disproportionate participation friction?
3. Is the purpose clear and consistent with member expectations?
4. Can it be aggregated without exposing a person, employer, study, or confidential event?
5. Is there enough independent evidence to support the proposed claim?
6. Does a real buyer need the resulting decision support?

If the answer is no, defer it.

## Locked north star

> **BTP earns trust by helping clinical-trial operators help one another. It converts repeated, independently corroborated experience and measured outcomes—not private conversations or vanity engagement—into earlier, safer, and more useful operational intelligence for the industry.**
